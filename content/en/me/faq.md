## Who is Gabriel?

Gabriel Chamorro Moris is a software engineer at Smartseer GmbH in Frankfurt, where he builds DMP, a personalisation and recommendation SaaS for the travel industry. He works across frontend, backend, product and design, and he was a professional trumpet player and music teacher before moving into software.

## What is your current role?

Software Engineer at Smartseer GmbH since May 2025. I build core features of the DMP platform, write vision documents and solution concepts for upcoming changes, design UI flows in Figma and take them to production. I also secured the API documentation portal with Logto and migrated the company website to WordPress, saving about €15,000 compared with an agency's quote.

## How many years of experience do you have?

I've worked as a web developer since June 2021: freelancing since then, an internship at Beehivve in 2022, almost three years as a frontend developer at Ameropa-Reisen (2022–2025) and, since 2025, as a software engineer at Smartseer.

## Why did you switch from music to software?

It started with my brass quintet, The Brass Collective. We needed a website to enter a competition, so I asked my cousin, a software engineer, for help. He didn't have much time, so instead of building it for us, he showed me how to build it myself, and I did. I enjoyed it so much that I kept learning with him, and then joined a full-stack bootcamp at Spiced Academy in Berlin. That website was my first web project, and the start of my career in software.

## What did you bring with you from music and teaching?

Years of teaching taught me to break complex ideas down into simple ones, and I use that every day: in UI design, where I turn complex features into intuitive flows, and when I explain technical decisions to clients and non-technical colleagues. Leading a brass quintet taught me to coordinate a team, communicate clearly and deliver under pressure.

## Do you have a computer science degree?

No. I have a master's degree in secondary-school teaching from the University of Santiago de Compostela. I learned software development through a full-stack bootcamp at Spiced Academy in Berlin (2021–2022), a Vue.js and TypeScript intensive training at Workshops.de, and since then through professional work and many side projects.

## Are you frontend or full-stack?

Frontend is my strongest area, but I work full stack. On the backend I've used Node.js, Express, PHP, PostgreSQL, Supabase and Firebase, including Cloud Functions, cron jobs and payment integrations with Stripe and PayPal. My side projects Demosratio, Refhub and MenuDiario are full-stack products I built end to end.

## React or Vue?

Both. I use React and Next.js most, at Smartseer and in my side projects. At Ameropa I built internal applications with Vue.js and TypeScript, and I've built personal projects with Vue 3, Pinia and Nuxt.

## What are you strongest at?

Taking a feature from an idea to production: turning a client request into a concept, designing it in Figma, building it as reusable, tested components, and shipping it. I care about clean, reusable code and interfaces that make complex things simple.

## Do you design as well?

Yes. I design UI flows and pages in Figma before building them, at work (overview tables, filters, wizard steps, previews, popups) and in my own projects. I also designed the logo and website of The Brass Collective.

## Have you built mobile apps?

Yes, with web technologies and Capacitor. MenuDiario, an app for daily restaurant menus, is published on Google Play and the App Store. Refhub, a referral-code hub, is on Google Play. Both share one codebase between the web and the native apps.

## What experience do you have with AI?

- **In products:** Demosratio uses the Gemini API to check parliamentary votes against election promises, extract promises from party programmes and write plain-language summaries, always grounded in official documents. Earlier projects used OpenAI to generate personalised cover letters (Jobs-AI) and social media posts (a Vue 3 app).
- **In my workflow:** I work AI-first. At Smartseer I wrote the AGENTS.md conventions that guide AI coding agents, and I use codebase-graph tooling to move quickly in large codebases. I've also experimented with building an MCP server.
- **In this portfolio:** the chatbot answers questions with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), running the model on the visitor's device when possible.

## How do you test your code?

I've used Jest and React Testing Library for unit and component tests, Vitest, Playwright and Cypress for end-to-end tests, and Husky pre-commit hooks to keep checks running on every commit.

## Do you have team and leadership experience?

Yes. I do code reviews and have mentored teammates at Ameropa, shared best practices with the developers at Beehivve, built MenuDiario with another developer through pull requests and reviews, and work closely with product, design and clients at Smartseer. Before software, I led and coordinated a brass quintet.

## Which languages do you speak?

Spanish and Galician natively, English and German at a professional level, and some basic Chinese.

I work in Frankfurt, Germany, and I've lived and worked in Berlin and Vienna. I've also done remote projects for companies and freelance clients.
